# XeroHoloGram v1.0.0 Release Notes

We are thrilled to announce the inaugural **v1.0.0** release of **`XeroHoloGram`** — the open-source, ultra-low-latency 3D Holographic AI Agent Harness and Universal Rigged-Character Engine.

## Highlights
- **105 Atomic Commits**: Architected and shipped across 7 comprehensive phases following strict Conventional Commits syntax.
- **Pepper's Ghost Physical Optics**: Householder reflection matrix algebra, real-time axis inversion ($S_x, S_y$), 45° Pythagorean FOV compensation, and trapezoidal keystone projective homography.
- **Universal Rig Engine & Bone Studio**: Drag-and-drop `.glb` / `.gltf` / `.vrm` auto-detection for Mixamo, VRoid Studio (VRM 0.x/1.0), Unreal Engine Manny/Quinn, and Blender Rigify onto a canonical 55-bone humanoid skeleton.
- **60Hz SO(3) Kinematics**: Lie algebra $\mathfrak{so}(3)$ critically damped quaternion spring-damper dynamics, cervical spine look-at gaze solver, Poisson eye saccades, and parametric diaphragmatic breathing.
- **Dual-Mode Viseme Lip-Sync**: Formant frequency energy binning ($F_1, F_2, F_3$) with asymmetric envelope follower driving 15 Preston Blair/Oculus visemes, plus timestamp-guided phoneme alignment.
- **Ultrafast Async Multiplexer Daemon**: Full-duplex WebSocket at `/ws/hologram` multiplexing binary PCM audio with 16-byte headers and JSON control packets, supporting sub-50ms instant barge-in cancellation.
- **Universal TTS Adapters**: Pre-built modular adapters for Edge-TTS, Kokoro-82M, ElevenLabs, Cartesia Sonic, OpenAI TTS-1, and local Piper.
